# The Handweaver’s Pattern Directory

Anne Dixon, *The Handweaver’s Pattern Directory: Over 600 Weaves for Four-Shaft Looms*. First North American edition, Interweave Press / Quarto, 2007. ISBN 9781596680401.

## Coverage

The catalog contains **571 usable numeric drafts**. Hand-manipulation-only ground drafts are omitted. It contains no PDFs, page images, or source-page placeholder entries.

## Conventions

- Page citations use printed page numbers; the supplied PDF has two preliminary pages before printed page 1.
- Threading is read right to left; shafts are numbered 1 at the bottom through 4 at the top. Lifting sequences are read bottom to top, joining continued diagrams in reading order.
- Numbers inside marked cells and numbered repeat brackets are expanded.
- Identical sheds share a treadle without changing the order of lifted shafts.
- Colors approximate the source yarns. Thickness ratios, reed spacing, sett, tension, and finishing affect the fabric beyond a flat drawdown.
- Balancing ends and excerpts are retained, so previews default to one repeat.
