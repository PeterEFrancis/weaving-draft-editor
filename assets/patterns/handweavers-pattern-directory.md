# The Handweaver’s Pattern Directory

Anne Dixon, *The Handweaver’s Pattern Directory: Over 600 Weaves for Four-Shaft Looms*. First North American edition, Interweave Press / Quarto, 2007. ISBN 9781596680401.

## Coverage

The catalog contains **627 patterns**, including separate color/lifting variants and every illustrated combination of shared threadings and lifting plans. It contains no PDFs, page images, or source-page placeholder entries. Introductory pages are not counted as patterns.

56 patterns require hand work in addition to their supporting draft. Their descriptions give the crossing, wrapping, pickup, inlay, or knotting instructions. A flat shaft drawdown cannot represent these manipulations or the finished three-dimensional fabric.

## Conventions

- Page citations use printed page numbers; the supplied PDF has two preliminary pages before printed page 1.
- Threading is read right to left; shafts are numbered 1 at the bottom through 4 at the top. Lifting sequences are read bottom to top, joining continued diagrams in reading order.
- Numbers inside marked cells and numbered repeat brackets are expanded. Unnumbered repeat units appear once unless a sample specifies otherwise.
- Added tabby follows the source’s position and color instructions. Tabby already included in the diagram is not inserted a second time.
- Identical sheds share a treadle without changing the order of lifted shafts. Closed-shed hand work uses an empty lift.
- Colors approximate the source yarns. Thickness ratios, reed spacing, sett, tension, hand work, and finishing affect the fabric beyond a flat drawdown. Follow each pattern’s notes.
- Balancing ends and excerpts are retained, so previews default to one repeat.

## Source ambiguities

- Printed p. 37, middle: the visible lifting grid is retained where its length differs from the caption.
- Printed p. 124, left: the scan has a damaged margin; the visible threading and its limitation are recorded in the pattern notes.
- Printed p. 129: the threading reaches the cropped edge; the visible excerpt and its limitation are identified in the pattern notes.
- Printed p. 189, bottom: the grid shows 14 picks in groups of eight and six, while the caption says eight in each block. The catalog preserves the visible grid.
- Some hand-worked motifs are described without a complete pickup chart. Their entries provide the published ground draft and manual instructions, rather than inventing a shaft-controlled motif.
