# A Handweaver’s Pattern Book

Marguerite Porter Davison, *A Handweaver’s Pattern Book*. Revised edition, eighteenth printing, 1977. The original edition was published in 1944.

## Coverage

The catalog covers all **417 named source entries** in the book’s pattern section: 40 named treadlings in Chapter I and 377 numbered threadings in Chapters II–XXI. Distinct printed treadlings for one named threading are separate editable variants that share a `sourceBaseId`, so they remain together in the library.

The book’s cloth photographs illustrate the printed drafts; a photograph is not counted as another draft unless the page also gives a distinct treadling or lift plan. The supplied scan, page images, and extracted text are not shipped with the website.

## Transcription conventions

- Page citations are printed page numbers. The pattern plates occupy printed pages 1–202.
- Davison’s threading is read from the upper right toward the left. Shaft 1 is the bottom row.
- The source diagrams use sinking-shed tie-ups. They are converted to raised-shaft lifts by taking the four-shaft complement, preserving the pattern-face interlacement in this library’s notation.
- Printed repeats and multipliers are expanded into explicit threading and lift arrays. Identical sheds may share a treadle in the editor without changing the lift sequence.
- Related treadling versions retain the named entry’s stable manifest ID in `sourceBaseId`; the variant ID remains unique.
- Numeric matrices are transcribed from the printed drafts, not reconstructed from the fabric photographs. Colors and yarn proportions are representative where the monochrome source does not specify them.
- The flat drawdown records interlacement. Sett, yarn, beat, finishing, and float deflection affect the woven cloth beyond this preview.
