# Handwoven Spring 2025 transcription

The issue supplies 22 distinct numeric drafts now transcribed in
`tools/handwoven-2025-ii-spring.json`. Source threading, tie-up, treadling,
numeric repeat brackets, and warp/weft color orders were extracted from the
PDF vectors and checked against rendered pages. Full warp counts and every
shaft's heddle count agree with the published counts.

- Sweet Linen and Lace Towels: all seven towel variations, including the
  explicitly directed color swaps for Towels 2 and 4 (p. 28).
- Lighter than Air Shawl: both 4-shaft and 2-shaft versions (p. 32).
- Cherry Blossom Scarf: 180 heddle assignments; the source's two doubled
  outer ends are retained as a loom instruction rather than extra shafts
  (p. 39).
- Create Sashiko Weaving Drafts with Ease: the separate, fully spelled-out
  35-end / 25-pick worked example, using the referenced project tie-up (p. 41).
- Sashiko Trails Table Linens: all four placemats and the full five-panel
  runner (pp. 44–45).
- Sugar Plum Lace Scarf: the complete 413-heddle threading and lace treadling
  (p. 50).
- Shades of Flax Towels: all three towels; continued outer threading and
  treadling brackets and the second-repeat jump are expanded (p. 54).
- Mosaic Tiles Scarf: 277 heddle assignments and the complete published
  2,309-pick color sequence (p. 58).
- City Circuits Scarf: 504 ends and 2,622 picks, including the initial,
  repeated center, shortened last center repeat, balance, and ending
  sections (p. 75).

Source limitations and representation:

- Length-only hem, border, and stripe brackets are represented by the
  displayed shed pair, with a note to repeat to the printed inch length.
  Numeric counts are expanded; blank separator grid rows are not picks.
- Floating selvedges are not assigned invented shafts. The library draft
  records the source heddle assignments; doubled selvedge ends and special
  denting still require the published loom instructions.
- Peach Harvest's multicolored striped yarn marker is approximated by one
  peach-gold palette color. Other palette colors approximate the source
  yarns. The sashiko design article has no yarn-color specification, so its
  example uses explicitly illustrative monochrome colors.
- Sunset Reflections (pp. 34–37) depends on hand-positioned inlay and provides
  no separate numeric draft beyond its plain-weave ground; omitted under the
  existing catalog convention for hand-manipulation-only grounds.
- Spring Breeze Jacket (pp. 66–69) depends on bead-leno thread crossings. Its
  supporting four-shaft shed chart alone cannot encode the crossed warp
  bundles; omitted under that same convention.
- Color Lab (pp. 22–24) shows illustrative fabric/draft previews rather than
  complete standalone numeric project instructions; no invented threading
  or color run was made from these previews.
- Subscriber-only projects on p. 77 are pictured and described, but their
  numeric drafts are not included in this supplied issue PDF. They are not
  transcribed from the promotional images.
