# Handwoven Fall 2025 transcription

The 32 drafts in `handwoven-2025-iv-fall.json` were transcribed from rendered
charts and their PDF vector marks using `build-handwoven-2025-iv-fall.py`.
Full published threading and warp-color counts are retained. Numeric brackets
are expanded; floating selvedges, finishing, and loom preparation are separate
from the shaft assignments.

- Zigzag Basket: 60 doubled warp ends and all 89 three-pick weft-color rows,
  including each printed repetition count (1,872 pattern picks).
- Plaid About You: 608 ends and all six passes through the continued weft
  color/treadling sequence (864 picks).
- Shadow Play: the complete 442-end warp color order, the X and O towels,
  continuous-pattern Towel 3, and the mixed X/O Towel 4. All ten motif colors
  for the numbered towels follow the printed color-order chart.
- Autumn Berries: 477 ends, the eight-shaft skeleton tie-up, all simultaneous
  treadle combinations, and 36 colored repeats in Coral, Bubblegum, Pink Haze,
  and Turmeric order. Opening and closing monochrome repeats are separate
  from the 24-pick accent unit. The draft contains 936 picks.
- Stormy Skies: four mostly solid towels, the fully specified plaid towel,
  and the silk-weft wrap on the same 415-end warp. The mostly solid towels
  use the article's suggested plaid-band sequence; the article explicitly
  permits choosing different bands. Lengths are expanded at 16 picks per
  inch. The plaid towel expands the central color-order bracket from 3x
  to 12x exactly as instructed.
- Prairie School Rug: all 1,080 warp colors and the thick/thin weft sequence.
  The relative yarn thickness distinguishes the fine binder from the
  tripled heavy weft; measured three-inch hems retain the displayed picks.
- Turned Twill on Three Blocks: both complete eight- and twelve-shaft study
  tie-ups. Their design units repeat four times for readable comparison.
- Ribbons and Blocks: both runners, using all 430 threaded ends and the
  complete block-repeat/color charts (1,500 and 1,600 picks). The published
  432-end color-order total includes two unthreaded floating selvedges;
  the numeric draft preserves the 430 actual heddle assignments.
- Choose a Path: the 360-end pillow threading, printed example band sequence,
  and all five numbered twill-circle studies. Doubled bouclé uses thicker
  yarn in the main pillow draft. Actual band widths and plain-weave lengths
  are chosen while weaving; the draft retains the displayed plain-weave picks.
- Family Dinner: the full 604-end warp, all six linen napkin colorways, and
  the cotton/cottolin towel. Numeric nested brackets and color sequences
  are expanded. Hems and white frames specified only in inches retain their
  displayed starter picks and must be woven to the printed measurements.
- Falling Leaves: all 247 threaded ends and their colors. Four complete leaf
  motifs are shown between the two-pick plain-weave ends; the original
  runner continues the motif to 75 inches.

Colors approximate the yarns. Software screenshots and subscriber-project
promotional photographs are not treated as complete numeric project drafts.
The source PDFs and rendered pages remain outside the published catalog.
